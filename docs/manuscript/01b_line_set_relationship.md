# Relationship to the line set {#sec:line-set-relationship}

Golden Line is the aspirational work among the founding four, whose declaration
lives in the companion work [`line_set`](https://github.com/docxology/line_set).
Red Line is the personal security boundary and explicit No document. Black
Line is positive operating discipline. White Line records absence, restraint,
and negative space. Golden Line does not copy their registries or evaluators,
and a directional finding is never a compliance verdict.

Current membership belongs to the [Line Set declaration](https://github.com/docxology/line_set);
the reader and Witness Register are companions, not instruments. Historically
the fifth work, `line_set` is a thin reader that declares the set and checks that
no two lines gave the same spelling to different things; it adds no substantive
instrument, and Golden Line does not import, depend on, or defer to it.

A useful direction does not show developed capacity: Green Line records that
separate question. A wish to sustain inquiry does not show a maintained
commitment: Blue Line records that question. A flourishing marker does not
show affected-party consent: Violet Line records that question. None of these
separate records is imported, scored, or overridden by Golden Line.

Copper records dimensioned finite resource use, outstanding commitments, and
attributed burdens, not aspiration priorities or evidence of fair allocation.
Iron compares recorded recovery exercises with scenario-specific targets, not
aspirational progress or a promise of future recovery. Neither supplies permission
or a compensating score. Violet, Copper, and Iron sources are access-controlled.

This is a declaration of independent scopes, not an installation report.
Sibling sources may be optional or access-controlled; links and citations do
not establish public availability. Golden Line runs without those sources.

## Note on the name

The name is not incidental. *Golden Line* openly echoes the yellowing stage of
the alchemical *magnum opus*, **citrinitas**, the dawning that classical schemes
place between the whitening and the reddening. The echo is meant in Carl Jung's
symbolic-psychological register, where the stages of the opus are read as a map
of individuation, and in that register only: it is a metaphor for a practice
turning toward what it is for, never an empirical, mystical, or causal claim
about matter, minds, or this instrument. The historical founding-four order
refuse, then method, then aspire, then absence, is functional and chosen for how
the instruments are actually used, and deliberately does not reenact the opus's
canonical sequence (nigredo → albedo → citrinitas → rubedo). Citrinitas here
simply marks Golden Line's place in the set: the yellowing where a disciplined
practice lifts its attention from how the work is done to what the work is worth.
The grounding citation is Jung's reading of the opus stages as figures for
individuation rather than as laboratory chemistry [@jung1953alchemy], and it is
carried here rather than deferred, so this paper does not depend on another
document to say what its own name means. The set-level version of the framing —
the two orders, the caveats, and the non-overlap contract — is declared in the
companion work [`line_set`](https://github.com/docxology/line_set); this paper
points there for the set, not for its own citation.
